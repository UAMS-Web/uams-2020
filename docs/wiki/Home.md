# UAMS 2020 theme

<!-- cspell:ignore UAMS -->

This wiki documents the UAMS 2020 WordPress theme for the people who build and edit sites with it: what the theme needs, how its settings, templates and blocks work, and what to check when something does not appear. The [README](https://github.com/UAMS-Web/uams-2020#readme) keeps the overview, installation, building from source, tests and continuous integration.

Written against v1.10.2.
